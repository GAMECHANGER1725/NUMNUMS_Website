  w7       2026-09-22 .. 2026-09-28   (current 7 days)

  w28      2026-09-01 .. 2026-09-28   (current 28 days)
  w28prev  2026-08-04 .. 2026-08-31   (previous 28 days, days 29-56 back)

  w6mo     2026-04-01 .. 2026-09-28   (181 days)
  w12mo    2025-09-29 .. 2026-09-28   (365 days)
  sep2026  2026-09-01 .. 2026-09-30   (calendar month, the complete prior month)
  aug2026  2026-08-01 .. 2026-08-31   (calendar month, for month-over-month)
GA4 windows use the SAME explicit dates, so GSC and GA4 are like-for-like and
  CROSS_SOURCE is a valid comparison. GA4 has no 3-day lag, so sep2026 is complete
  in GA4 and truncated in GSC — this matters, see the three warnings below.

*** THREE MEASUREMENT WARNINGS THAT CHANGE HOW EVERY TREND BELOW READS ***

WARNING 1 — SEPTEMBER IS 28 DAYS OF GSC DATA, NOT 30.
  GSC w28 (09-01..09-28) and GSC sep2026 (09-01..09-30) return the IDENTICAL totals
  (796 clicks / 29793 impressions). 2026-09-29 and 2026-09-30 carry zero rows, which
  is the reporting lag, not a traffic collapse. So the headline
  "Sep -14.3% clicks vs Aug" compares 28 days against 31 days.
  PER-DAY, the honest figure:
    GSC clicks/day  Sep 796/28 = 28.43   Aug 929/31 = 29.97   -> -5.1%
    GSC impr/day    Sep 29793/28 = 1064  Aug 35018/31 = 1130  -> -5.8%
    GA4 organic sessions/day (no lag) Sep 1017/30 = 33.9  Aug 1096/31 = 35.4 -> -4.1%
  Two independent sources agree on roughly -4% to -6% per day. That is the real
  month-over-month movement. The -14% headline is an artifact. Do not act on -14%.

WARNING 2 — THE 12-MONTH WINDOW CONTAINS NO MORE DATA THAN THE 6-MONTH WINDOW.
  w6mo and w12mo are byte-identical on every metric (2890 clicks / 103682
  impressions / 2.79% / 11.3) and GA4 likewise (6145 sessions). GSC holds no usable
  data before ~2026-04-01 for this property. Google also flags an impressions
  logging error for 2025-05-13..2026-04-27. Therefore: 12-month trend and
  seasonality CANNOT be assessed, and any "year on year" claim is unsupported.
  Treat w6mo as the longest honest window. First real YoY comparison: 2027-04.
