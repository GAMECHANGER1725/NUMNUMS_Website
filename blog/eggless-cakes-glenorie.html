Why Is There No Eggless Bakery Actually In Glenorie?

Glenorie's own village centre runs to a general store, a public school, and a scattering of rural-supply businesses — there simply isn't the retail density to support a specialty bakery. The Hills Shire Council classifies Glenorie as part of its rural and environmental conservation land, dominated by acreage and hobby-farm blocks rather than shopping strips. That's exactly why families here end up driving to Riverstone, Dural, or further afield for anything beyond daily essentials.

Running a dedicated eggless kitchen takes real investment — separate storage, reformulated recipes for every flavour, and a lot of trial and error to get texture right without the binding and rise eggs normally provide. Most small rural bakeries won't take that on for a niche customer base. It's genuinely all we do at Num Num's, which is exactly why nobody nearby offers it.

Are Eggless Cakes Safe for Egg Allergy Sufferers Near Glenorie?

Egg is one of the eight priority food allergens under Australian law, according to Food Standards Australia New Zealand (FSANZ) — strict labelling rules apply, and cross-contamination from egg-based products baked nearby is a genuine risk in any mixed kitchen. At Num Num's, eggs simply aren't stocked or used anywhere in the Riverstone kitchen, so that cross-contamination pathway doesn't exist in the first place.

That said, honesty matters more than marketing here. Being egg-free is not the same as being allergen-free. Our cakes still contain dairy — milk powder and butter — and some flavours involve nuts, while standard cakes contain wheat. For a severe or anaphylactic egg allergy, we recommend following your own medical or ASCIA-aligned advice and telling us your full dietary needs when you order, so we can talk through the specific flavour and any cross-allergen questions before you commit.

This same egg-free framework benefits several other dietary groups common across the Hills Shire:

  • Hindu vegetarian households: many lacto-vegetarian traditions exclude eggs entirely, and our full menu already fits that.
  • Sikh families: some Sikh dietary codes exclude eggs; the entire Num Num's range is compatible on that basis.
  • Jain households: Jain dietary practice excludes eggs; every flavour we make already qualifies.
  • Vegan or plant-based families: note our cakes contain dairy, so we're not a vegan bakery — but we are fully egg-free.

We are not a certified kosher, halal, or Monash-certified low FODMAP kitchen — if any of those frameworks matter for your event, message us directly and we'll walk through the ingredient list for your chosen flavour before you order.
