Are Eggless Cakes Suitable for Dietary and Cultural Needs Near Berkshire Park?

According to Food Standards Australia New Zealand (FSANZ), eggs are one of the eight priority food allergens requiring strict labelling under Australian law, and cross-contamination in a mixed bakery is a genuine risk for egg-allergic customers. At Num Num's, eggs aren't used in any product, preparation area or piece of equipment on-site — it's a whole-kitchen policy, not just a recipe swap.

That distinction matters for a wide range of households across the catchment, including Hindu vegetarian families, Sikh households and anyone managing a diagnosed egg allergy. It's worth being upfront about what egg-free does and doesn't cover, though: our cakes still contain dairy (milk powder, butter), standard sponges contain wheat, and some flavours involve nuts. We're an egg-free bakery, not an allergen-free one, and we're not a certified kosher or halal kitchen either.

If you're managing a severe or anaphylactic egg allergy, tell us your exact needs when you order and follow your own doctor's or NSW Health-aligned advice on top of that — we'll never promise medical-grade allergen safety beyond the egg-free kitchen itself. For anything beyond eggs, message us on WhatsApp before ordering and we'll walk through the full ingredient list for your chosen flavour.
