Are Eggless Cakes Suitable for McGraths Hill's Dietary Needs?

Egg-free baking covers a real need across the Hawkesbury's mixed households, even in a small suburb like McGraths Hill. According to Food Standards Australia New Zealand (FSANZ), eggs are one of the eight priority food allergens in Australia, with strict labelling rules attached — for egg-allergic families, cross-contamination from a bakery that uses eggs elsewhere on-site is a real and reasonable concern.

At Num Num's Bakery, eggs aren't used in any product, preparation area, or piece of equipment on-site. That's not just a recipe choice — it's a whole-kitchen policy. Isn't that the level of certainty most egg-allergic families are actually looking for? It also matters for:

  • Families managing infant or childhood egg allergy — around 9% of Australian infants have an egg allergy, according to Allergy & Anaphylaxis Australia, making a fully egg-free kitchen far simpler than a "please substitute" request.
  • Vegetarian households — some vegetarian dietary traditions exclude eggs; a whole-kitchen egg-free policy removes that question entirely rather than requiring a special order.
  • Halal-observant families — our fully egg-free kitchen sidesteps a whole category of cross-contamination questions when it comes to ingredient sourcing and preparation.
  • Vegan or plant-based families — note that our cakes still contain dairy (milk powder, butter); we're not a vegan bakery, but we are fully egg-free throughout.

If you have allergen questions beyond eggs, contact us directly via WhatsApp before ordering, and we'll walk you through a full ingredient breakdown for your chosen flavour. For anaphylactic egg allergy specifically, we recommend following your own medical or Allergy & Anaphylaxis Australia-aligned advice and telling us your exact needs before you order — we can't promise medical-grade allergen control beyond our egg-free kitchen, but we'll be upfront about exactly what that does and doesn't cover.
