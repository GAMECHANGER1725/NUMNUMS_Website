Why Order a Mini Cake Instead of a Standard Size?

A standard 8-inch cake serves roughly 12-14 people, which is exactly right for a full birthday party but genuinely wasteful for a gathering of four or five. Ordering a size that matches your actual guest count means less food waste, a fresher cake with no days-old leftovers sitting in the fridge, and often a lower price than a full-size custom order.

A mini cake isn't a downgrade in presentation. A 4-inch or 6-inch cake can carry the same fondant detailing, piping work, or photo-print finish as a much larger cake — the design simply scales down to the smaller canvas. Many of our customers choose mini specifically because a smaller, more detailed cake reads as more considered than a plain, oversized one.

Food waste is a genuine cost, not just a vague guilt. According to OzHarvest's "Half-Eaten" household food waste research, 2025, Australian households waste 2.46 million tonnes of food every year, costing roughly $2,000-2,500 per household annually. A cake sized for the actual guest list is a small, practical way to avoid adding to that total.

In 2021, 25% of NSW households contained a single person, according to ABS Census household size data. That's roughly one in four households where a standard 8-inch celebration cake produces far more leftovers than anyone can realistically eat before it goes stale.

What Size Cake Do I Need for a Small Gathering?

A 4-inch round cake serves 2-4 people with generous slices, making it the right choice for a quiet birthday, a couple's anniversary, or a solo celebration shared with one or two close friends. It's also a popular "smash cake" alternative for a baby's first birthday photoshoot, without the waste of a full-size cake.

A 6-inch round cake steps up to 6-8 people, suiting a small family dinner, a close-friend group celebration, or a compact office team marking a birthday or work anniversary. Both sizes are available in every one of our 15 eggless flavours, and both can be custom-decorated to match your theme, colour palette, or written message.

Should I Order a Mini Cake or a Box of Cupcakes Instead?

A mini cake and a batch of cupcakes solve slightly different problems, and the right choice depends on the moment you're marking. From our order history, customers choose a mini cake specifically when there's a cake-cutting moment planned — candles, a photo, a shared first slice — because a single small cake still delivers that centrepiece feeling a scattered box of cupcakes doesn't.

Cupcakes make more sense when guests are arriving at different times, or when portion control matters more than ceremony — a workplace morning tea, for instance, where people graze rather than gather. See our comparison of eggless cake versus cupcakes for a fuller breakdown, and our guide to cake versus a full dessert table if you're weighing up a bigger spread.

For most small gatherings we cater, a single 4-inch or 6-inch cake wins out. It keeps the ceremony of cutting a cake together, without the volume of a size built for a much bigger room. If you're genuinely torn, some customers split the difference — a 4-inch centrepiece cake for the cutting moment, paired with a small tray of matching-flavour treats for anyone who wants seconds.

Are Mini Eggless Cakes Suitable for Allergy and Dietary Needs?

Egg-free baking directly addresses one of Australia's most common food allergens, regardless of cake size. According to Food Standards Australia New Zealand (FSANZ), eggs are one of the eight priority food allergens under Australian law. A smaller cake for a smaller gathering doesn't mean a smaller allergy risk — cross-contamination matters just as much.

At Num Num's Bakery, eggs are not used in any product, preparation area, or piece of equipment on-site, at any size. That covers egg allergy sufferers, Hindu vegetarian and Sikh households that exclude eggs, and anyone avoiding eggs for personal reasons. Our cakes do contain dairy (milk powder, butter), so they're not suitable for a strictly vegan or dairy-free guest.

For severe or anaphylactic egg allergy, state your needs clearly when ordering and follow your own medical or ASCIA-aligned advice. We can confirm exactly what's in each flavour, even at a small cake scale, but we can't offer medical-grade guarantees beyond our egg-free kitchen practice.
