Are Eggless Cakes Suitable for Dietary and Cultural Needs in Meadowbank?

Egg-free baking aligns with several dietary frameworks common in Meadowbank's mixed community. According to Food Standards Australia New Zealand (FSANZ), eggs are one of the eight priority food allergens in Australia, with strict labelling laws in place. For egg-allergic families, cross-contamination from a bakery that uses eggs in other products is a genuine risk.

At Num Num's Bakery, eggs are not used in any product, any preparation area, or any equipment on-site. That distinction matters for families managing egg allergy, and it also matters for:

  • Hindu vegetarian households — many Hindu dietary traditions exclude eggs as part of lacto-vegetarian practice.
  • Sikh and Jain families — several Sikh and Jain dietary codes exclude eggs, and our entire menu is compatible on the egg axis.
  • Halal-observant households — eggs from conventional sources are generally halal, but cross-contamination with non-halal ingredients in a mixed bakery can be a concern. Note that we are not a certified halal kitchen; our egg-free environment removes only the egg-related cross-contamination question.
  • Vegan or plant-based families — our cakes contain dairy (milk powder, butter), so we're not a vegan bakery, but we are fully egg-free.

If you have specific allergen questions beyond eggs, contact us directly via WhatsApp before ordering, and we'll give you a complete ingredient breakdown for your chosen flavour. For general food safety guidance, the NSW Health food safety pages are a useful reference for anyone managing a household allergy.
