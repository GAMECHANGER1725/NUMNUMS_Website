Are Eggless Cakes Safe for Egg Allergies and Other Dietary Needs?

According to Food Standards Australia New Zealand (FSANZ), egg is one of the eight priority food allergens requiring strict labelling under Australian food law. At Num Num's Bakery, eggs are never used in any product, preparation area, or piece of equipment on-site — this removes the cross-contamination risk entirely, which matters enormously for families managing an egg allergy near Shanes Park, Colebee, or Marsden Park.

Personal Experience.

Being 100% egg-free does not mean allergen-free across the board. Our cakes contain dairy, including milk powder and butter, and standard cakes contain wheat. Some flavours and decorations may involve nuts, so if you're managing a severe or anaphylactic egg allergy, tell us your exact needs when you order and continue to follow your own doctor's advice, not ours alone.

Being entirely egg-free also aligns naturally with several dietary practices common across Sydney's north-west, including Hindu vegetarian and Sikh households that avoid eggs as part of their food traditions, and Jain households, whose dietary code also excludes eggs. We're not a certified vegan bakery — the dairy content rules that out — but on the specific question of eggs, the entire kitchen qualifies. If you have questions about a specific flavour's full ingredient list, message us directly before ordering and we'll walk you through it.
