Are Eggless Cakes Suitable for Dietary and Cultural Needs Near North Richmond?

Egg-free baking lines up with several dietary frameworks common across the Hawkesbury's diverse but tight-knit community. According to Food Standards Australia New Zealand (FSANZ), eggs are one of the eight priority food allergens in Australia, with strict labelling laws attached — and for egg-allergic families, cross-contamination from a bakery that also handles eggs elsewhere is a genuine, ongoing risk.

At Num Num's Bakery, eggs are never used in any product, any preparation area, or any piece of equipment on-site. That isn't just a recipe choice — it's the whole kitchen environment. It also matters for:

  • Hindu vegetarian households — many Hindu dietary traditions exclude eggs as a form of lacto-vegetarian practice.
  • Sikh families — some Sikh dietary codes exclude eggs, and North Richmond and the wider Hawkesbury are home to established Sikh and Hindu communities.
  • Halal-observant households — eggs are generally halal, but cross-contamination with non-halal ingredients in a mixed bakery can be a concern; our fully egg-free kitchen sidesteps that question entirely.
  • Jain households — Jain dietary practices exclude eggs; our entire menu is Jain-compatible on the egg axis.
  • Vegan or plant-based families — note our cakes contain dairy (milk powder, butter); we're not a vegan bakery, and we're not a certified kosher kitchen either, but every cake is fully egg-free.

If you have specific allergen questions beyond eggs, contact us directly via WhatsApp before ordering. We'll give you a complete ingredient breakdown for your chosen flavour, and for severe or anaphylactic allergies we always recommend following your own medical or ASCIA-aligned advice alongside anything we tell you.
