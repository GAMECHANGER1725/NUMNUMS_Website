Are Eggless Cakes Suitable for Dietary and Cultural Needs in Lalor Park?

Egg-free baking lines up with several dietary needs common across Lalor Park's mixed community. According to Food Standards Australia New Zealand (FSANZ), egg is one of the ten priority food allergens with mandatory labelling requirements — and for egg-allergic families, cross-contamination at a bakery that also handles eggs elsewhere is a real concern.

At Num Num's Bakery, eggs are never brought on-site — not in the recipes, the prep areas, or the equipment. That's a kitchen-wide commitment, not a recipe tweak, and it matters for more than one dietary group:

  • Egg-allergic households: a fully egg-free kitchen removes the cross-contamination pathway that a shared kitchen can't.
  • Hindu vegetarian households: many lacto-vegetarian dietary traditions exclude eggs entirely.
  • Sikh and Jain families: several dietary codes in both communities also exclude eggs.
  • Vegan or plant-based families: note our cakes still contain dairy (milk powder, butter) — we're egg-free, not vegan.

Severe or anaphylactic egg allergy needs extra care. Please tell us about it when you order, and follow your own medical or ASCIA-aligned advice regardless of what we tell you about our kitchen — we're happy to walk through the full ingredient list for your chosen flavour before you commit. If you have questions beyond eggs, message us directly on WhatsApp before ordering.
