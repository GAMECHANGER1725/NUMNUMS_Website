Why Are Oil-Based Sponges Trending in 2026?

Bakery industry coverage of 2026 trends points to oil-based sponges and crunchy-creamy textures as leading shifts away from traditional butter-cream cake construction, according to Tastewise's 2026 cake trends analysis. Oil-based sponges tend to stay moist for longer and produce a denser, more tender crumb than butter-based recipes. That's exactly the texture eggless bakers have spent years engineering, because oil — not egg or butter — is typically what replaces the binding and moisture eggs would otherwise provide.

In other words, a trend the wider cake industry is treating as new territory is something eggless bakeries have quietly been doing by necessity for years. That's not a coincidence — it's a genuine technical overlap between "trending" and "how eggless cakes have always worked."

Industry analysis identifies oil-based sponges and crunchy-creamy textures among the leading cake trends for 2026 (Tastewise, Best Cake Trends 2026). Eggless recipes are built around oil rather than eggs or butter by necessity, meaning dedicated eggless bakeries are structurally aligned with this trend rather than adapting to it.

Which Flavours Are Trending for 2026 Celebrations?

46% of consumers are actively seeking exotic flavour combinations in 2026, with South Asian flavours and cross-cultural pairings gaining particular traction, according to Tastewise's 2026 birthday cake trends report. That trend lines up directly with three flavours already on our permanent menu: Rasmalai, Mango, and Lychee, all of which draw on South and Southeast Asian flavour traditions rather than a Western vanilla-chocolate default.

Chocolate remains the single most common flavour choice nationally, appearing in around 18% of tracked cake orders, so it's not disappearing — but the growth is in pairing familiar bases with less expected flavours. Ferrero Rocher (chocolate-hazelnut) and Tiramisu (coffee-forward) both sit in that "familiar but elevated" space that 2026 trend data consistently points to.

What Design Trends Are Shaping 2026 Cake Orders?

Message-driven cakes with personalised text, and simplified vintage-inspired buttercream piping, are two of the strongest design trends for 2026, according to Anges de Sucre's 2026 cake trends guide. Both trends favour thoughtful, personal touches over elaborate structural work — good news if you want a design that feels current without a long lead time.

Lambeth piping and other vintage-inspired decorating styles, drawing on 1970s and '80s aesthetics, are also making a comeback across the industry. For a custom order, that might mean requesting a simpler shell-border or rosette pattern rather than a fully sculpted design — a look that reads as intentional rather than dated, and usually takes less time to prepare too.

From our own custom order conversations this year, requests for a short personal message piped directly onto the cake — rather than a printed edible image — have become noticeably more common. It's a simpler ask for us to fulfil and it tends to look more considered on the finished cake.

Does the Health-Conscious Baking Trend Apply to Eggless Cakes?

Partially. Industry coverage describes 2026 as a year of "health-conscious indulgence" — growing demand for allergen-conscious, portion-controlled, and plant-forward bakery options, according to Bakery and Snacks' 2026 trends coverage. Egg-free baking is genuinely allergen-conscious — it removes one of Australia's eight priority allergens entirely — but it's worth being clear about what that does and doesn't mean.

Our cakes are not low-sugar, low-fat, or otherwise health-optimised; they're a treat food made without eggs. Egg-free addresses a specific allergy and dietary need, not general health goals, so don't assume "eggless" means "healthier" in a broader sense — it means one specific ingredient is absent, done properly, in a dedicated egg-free kitchen. If you're managing a broader dietary requirement beyond eggs, ask us directly rather than assuming it's covered.

Is Sustainability Shaping 2026 Cake Trends Too?

Yes — portion-controlled and single-serve formats are one of the quieter trends running through 2026 bakery coverage, alongside the more visible flavour and design shifts, according to Bakery and Snacks' 2026 trends coverage. Smaller gatherings and more frequent, lower-waste celebrations are pushing demand towards right-sized cakes rather than defaulting to the largest option on the menu.

That shift is practical as much as it is environmental — a cake sized correctly for the guest list means less food waste and a better price-per-guest outcome. If you're planning an intimate gathering of 8–10 people, ask us about our smaller round sizes rather than automatically ordering the size you'd choose for a 30-person party. It's a small change that adds up.

How to Order the Right Size for 2026-Style Smaller Gatherings

  • 8–10 guests: A 6-inch round is usually enough, with a little left over.
  • 15–20 guests: An 8-inch round is the standard choice for most birthday-sized gatherings.
  • 25–30 guests: Step up to a 10-inch round or consider a two-tier design.
  • 30+ guests: A two-tier or sheet cake format serves a larger crowd efficiently.
