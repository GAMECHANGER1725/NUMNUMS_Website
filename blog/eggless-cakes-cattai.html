Are Eggless Cakes Safe for Allergies and Dietary Needs in Cattai?

Egg allergy affects roughly 9% of Australian infants, according to Allergy & Anaphylaxis Australia, and it's one of the eight priority allergens tracked under Australian food standards. At Num Num's Bakery, eggs are never used in any product, preparation area, or piece of equipment on-site, which matters more than a simple recipe swap for families managing a genuine allergy.

It's important to be upfront about what "100% eggless" does and doesn't cover. Our cakes still contain dairy — milk powder and butter are standard ingredients — and some flavours may involve nuts, so this is not a nut-free or dairy-free kitchen. Standard cake bases also contain wheat. If you're managing a severe or anaphylactic allergy of any kind, tell us the specifics when you order, and follow your own doctor's or Food Standards Australia New Zealand-aligned advice rather than relying on our labelling alone.

  • Egg allergy. The entire kitchen is egg-free, removing the main cross-contamination risk from eggs specifically.
  • Hindu and Jain vegetarian households. Many vegetarian dietary traditions exclude eggs; our full range qualifies on that measure.
  • Sikh dietary practices. Some Sikh households also avoid eggs, and every flavour we sell meets that requirement.
  • Vegan or plant-based diets. Note that our cakes contain dairy, so they are not suitable for a vegan diet despite being egg-free.

For anything beyond eggs, message us on WhatsApp before you order so we can walk through a full ingredient breakdown for your chosen flavour.
