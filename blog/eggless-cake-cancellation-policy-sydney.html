Eggless Cake Cancellation and Rescheduling Policy: What Sydney Customers Need to Know

By Tarun Patel 23 August 2026 11 min read Ordering Policy · Eggless Cakes

Life does not always cooperate with the party planner. A guest list changes, a venue reschedules, or someone comes down with the flu two days before the big day, and suddenly you are staring at a custom eggless cake order wondering what happens next. Because every cake at Num Num's Bakery is baked fresh to order rather than pulled from a display fridge, cancellations and date changes work a little differently than they would at a supermarket bakery.

This guide explains, in plain language, how far ahead you can cancel or reschedule without losing your payment, what changes once your cake enters production, and exactly what to have ready when you message us on WhatsApp. It is the same policy we apply to every custom order across our Harris Park and Riverstone kitchens, for every flavour on our 15-flavour eggless menu.

Quick Summary
  • Reschedule with 48+ hours notice and there is no penalty.
  • Custom cakes are made fresh and cannot be resold, so late cancellations may not be refundable.
  • Large or elaborate orders may need a partial deposit.
  • To reschedule, WhatsApp us your order date and preferred new date.
  • Festivals like Diwali, Eid, and Christmas need 4–5 days notice.

How Far in Advance Can You Cancel or Reschedule an Eggless Cake Order?

Cancel or reschedule with at least 48 hours notice and you will not pay a cancellation fee — that is the same minimum window we ask for when you first place an order. From our order records across Harris Park and Riverstone, the majority of change requests come in well before that cutoff, which is exactly why we can absorb them without extra cost to either side.

For elaborate or tiered custom designs, and during busy periods such as Diwali, Eid, Christmas, or NSW school holidays, we recommend 4–5 days notice for both the original order and any change. That extra buffer gives us room to reorganise ingredients and decorating time without rushing another customer's order to make space for yours.

Num Num's Bakery requires a minimum of 48 hours notice for standard eggless cake orders, and 4–5 days for elaborate custom designs or during festival periods such as Diwali, Eid, and Christmas. Cancelling or rescheduling within that same window keeps your order fee-free in almost every case.

What Happens If You Cancel Close to Your Pickup Date?

Cancelling inside 48 hours of pickup, once ingredients have been portioned and decorating has begun, means a full refund is unlikely because that cake cannot simply be resold to someone else. We would still rather find a workable solution than leave you with nothing, so message us the moment plans change and we will talk through the options honestly.

Original Data.

From our order history across both locations, last-minute cancellations inside the 48-hour window make up a small share of total requests — most customers who need a change reach out days ahead, and the ones who cannot are usually dealing with a genuine emergency rather than a change of mind.

Where a cake has not yet gone into production — say, you message us six hours after confirming, before we have shopped for ingredients — we can usually cancel with no cost at all. The closer you get to your slot, the more of the order is already locked in on our side: ingredients bought, a decorating window blocked out, and another customer's request potentially turned away to fit yours.

A genuine no-show is treated separately from a cancellation. If you simply do not arrive to collect a completed cake and never message us, that is different from telling us in advance you cannot make it. Reaching out — even a short message on the morning of pickup — always puts you in a better position than silence, because it gives us the chance to hold the cake a little longer or discuss options.

Why Can't Custom Cakes Be Resold or Refunded Once Production Starts?

A custom eggless cake cannot be resold once it is decorated because it is built around your flavour choice, size, message, and design brief — nobody else's order matches it. This is the core reason our policy tightens the closer you get to pickup, and it is standard practice across the custom cake industry, not something unique to us.

Under Australian Consumer Law, businesses are not generally required to offer a change-of-mind refund on goods made to a customer's specification once work has genuinely begun, though normal consumer guarantees around quality still apply. The ACCC's guidance on repairs, replacements and refunds and the NSW Fair Trading page on refunds and consumer guarantees both explain this distinction in plain language, and we encourage customers to read them if they want the fuller legal picture.

A custom-made eggless cake cannot be resold once decorating has started, which is why change-of-mind refunds become unlikely close to pickup — a principle consistent with how Australian Consumer Law treats made-to-order goods generally (ACCC). We still assess every case individually rather than applying a blanket rule.

How Do You Reschedule an Eggless Cake Order Instead of Cancelling?

Reschedule by messaging us your original pickup date and your preferred new date, and in most cases we can shift your order without any extra charge. Rescheduling is almost always the better option compared to cancelling outright, because it keeps your deposit or payment applied to a future date instead of walking away from it entirely.

Illness, sudden bad weather, and event postponements are the three reasons we hear most often, and all three are handled the same way — as early notice as possible, followed by a fast written confirmation of the new slot.

Personal Experience.

We have had customers message us mid-storm warning asking to move a Saturday pickup to Sunday, and families reschedule a first birthday cake by a week after a child came down with a fever. In every case we have been able to find a new slot because the request came in with real lead time, not on the morning of the original pickup.

Do You Need to Pay a Deposit for a Custom Eggless Cake?

A partial deposit may be requested for large, multi-tier, or heavily customised cake designs, mainly to secure your production slot and cover the specialty ingredients bought specifically for your order. Standard single-flavour cakes in our regular sizes typically do not require one.

We will always tell you upfront, before you confirm, whether your specific order needs a deposit. If it does, that amount is what is genuinely at risk if you cancel very late — it is not a hidden fee, it reflects the cost we have already committed on your behalf. See our eggless cake price guide for how sizing and design complexity affect overall cost.

What Should You Have Ready When You Message Us to Reschedule?

Have your original pickup date, your flavour and size, and your preferred new date ready before you message +61 425 697 725 on WhatsApp — that single message usually gets your reschedule confirmed within the same conversation. The less back-and-forth needed, the sooner you get certainty.

  1. Original order details: the pickup date and location you first booked, either Harris Park or Riverstone.
  2. Flavour and size: which of our 15 eggless flavours you chose and the cake size or tier count.
  3. Reason for the change: a brief note helps us understand urgency, though it is not required.
  4. Your new preferred date: ideally with a backup date in case your first choice is already booked.
  5. Any design changes: tell us now if the new date also means a different message, colour, or theme.

Every cake we bake is dairy-based, since we use milk powder and butter in place of eggs, and some flavours and decorations involve nuts — so if your reschedule also changes who is attending, mention any new allergy or dietary need at the same time. Standard cakes also contain wheat. Our kitchen is 100% egg-free, not allergen-free beyond eggs, and we disclose that plainly per Food Standards Australia New Zealand's priority allergen guidance.

What Are Your Consumer Rights When Cancelling a Custom-Made Cake?

You keep the same core consumer guarantees on a custom cake as on any purchase — the cake must be of acceptable quality and match what was agreed — but a "change of mind" is treated differently once a made-to-order item is in production. That distinction is a standard feature of Australian Consumer Law for custom or bespoke goods generally, not a policy we invented ourselves.

Unique Insight.

Most disputes we have seen over the years come down to timing rather than disagreement over the rule itself — customers who message us early are almost always accommodated, and the rare frustration comes from a cancellation attempted after a cake was already decorated. Clear, early communication resolves nearly every case before it becomes a dispute at all.

If you want to understand the general legal framework in more depth, the NSW Fair Trading refunds and guarantees page and the ACCC's consumer guarantees guidance both cover how custom-made goods are treated differently from off-the-shelf products. We are always happy to talk through your specific situation directly rather than pointing you to a policy page and nothing else.

We also confirm every order, and every change to an order, in writing over WhatsApp rather than relying on a verbal chat at the counter. That written record protects both sides: you have a clear reference for what was agreed, and we have a clear reference for what to prepare. If a dispute ever does arise, that message thread is usually enough to resolve it quickly and fairly.
