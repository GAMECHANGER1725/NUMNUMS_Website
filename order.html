Why We Bake Without Eggs

100% Eggless. Not Just a Label. Our Promise.

At Num Num's Bakery, we have never used eggs in any cake we bake. Not in our recipes, not in our kitchen. That means every single item on our menu is safe for vegetarian, Jain-friendly, and halal-friendly families without having to ask twice.

eco

Suitable for Everyone

Our cakes are proudly 100% eggless, making them perfect for vegetarian families, those following a Jain diet, halal-friendly celebrations, and anyone with an egg allergy or intolerance. No substitutions needed. The whole menu is inclusive by design.

cake

No Compromise on Taste

Eggless does not mean dry or dense. We have refined our recipes over years to achieve the same soft, moist crumb and rich flavour you would expect from a traditional bakery. Our 15 available flavours, from classic Chocolate to Rasmalai and Ferrero Rocher, each using carefully balanced ingredients to deliver exceptional taste and texture.

verified

Made Fresh to Order

We do not pre-bake and store cakes. Every order is made fresh once you confirm, ensuring maximum freshness at pick up. We ask for at least 48 hours notice so we can prepare your cake properly. No rushed shortcuts.
